Privacy Policy & Security Architecture

Last Updated: November 5, 2026
Status: Architecture-Verified (Local-First Platform)

We believe that your daily routines, mental health goals, sobriety tracking, and personal habits are yours alone. Our software is engineered from the ground up on a local-first, zero-knowledge architecture. This means we do not, and physically cannot, read, aggregate, sell, or monetize your habit logs.

Unlike traditional applications that sync your raw data to a centralized cloud database, this app executes and secures everything directly on your physical hardware.

1. Data Minimization Matrix

We design our application around the principle of absolute data minimization. If data is not strictly required for the core execution of your habit logs, it never comes into existence.

What We Never See vs. What Your Device Secures

What we never see vs. what your device secures
Data Category What We See (Our Servers) What Your Device Secures (Local Sandbox)
Identity & Authentication Nothing. We do not offer or require account registration, email sign-ups, or OAuth social logins. Nothing. The app operates entirely accountless.
Habit Metadata & Logs Nothing. We have zero access to your habit names, schedules, or completion histories. Full History. Encrypted relational tables tracking your habits, custom categories, completion checks, and streak counts.
Behavioral Timestamps Nothing. Date Buckets Only. Exact execution timestamps are truncated to localized date strings (YYYY-MM-DD) to eliminate fine-grained behavioral profiling.
Diagnostics & Crash Logs Nothing. We do not ship proprietary analytics packages or background tracking scripts. Volatile Memory. Diagnostic traces are maintained strictly within your temporary system memory bounds to resolve local exceptions.

2. On-Device Storage & Cryptographic Layer

Your data is protected at rest within an isolated application sandbox. It cannot be read by other applications on your device or intercepted via local storage extractions.

  • Database Encryption: All transactional data, habits, and historical metrics are managed inside a dedicated SQLite instance compiled with SQLCipher. The database is fully encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • Hardware-Backed Key Derivation: The unique 256-bit symmetric encryption key used to lock your local database is never stored in plain text.
    • iOS: Derived and stored inside the iOS Secure Enclave using kSecAttrAccessibleAfterFirstUnlockThisDeviceOnly. This configuration prevents the key from being bundled into unencrypted cloud snapshots or extracted off-chip.
    • Android: Managed via the Android Keystore system, utilizing hardware-backed Trusted Execution Environments (TEE) or StrongBox Keymaster modules where physically supported by the device.

3. Zero-Knowledge Synchronization & Backups

To ensure you do not lose your streaks when switching devices, the app utilizes an end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) cloud synchronization system.
